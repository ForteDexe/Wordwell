Optional English spelling uses nspell 2.1.5 (MIT),
https://github.com/wooorm/nspell, and is-buffer 2.0.5 (MIT),
https://github.com/feross/is-buffer. Dictionary data comes from dictionary-en
4.0.0 and dictionary-en-gb 3.0.0 (MIT AND BSD),
https://github.com/wooorm/dictionaries, based on the English Hunspell / SCOWL
dictionaries. Wordwell merges the US and British dictionary entries while
keeping their shared affix rules. Complete licenses and original notices
are included in vendor/spelling/.
